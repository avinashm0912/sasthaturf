TURF RULES

Play fair. Keep it safe.

Please follow these rules so everyone enjoys their game.

🚫

No alcohol

Alcohol and intoxicated players are not allowed.

🔇

No shouting

Keep the noise down. Cheer, but don't scream.

🏘️

Respect the surroundings

Do not disturb neighbours or the nearby area.

🗣️

No bad words

Abusive or foul language is not allowed.

🤝

No fighting

Fights or aggressive behaviour lead to immediate removal.

🚭

No smoking

Smoking and tobacco are not allowed inside the turf.

🗑️

Keep it clean

Use the dustbins. Do not litter on the turf.

👟

Proper footwear

Wear turf or sports shoes. No spikes or metal studs.

⏰

Respect your time

Start and finish on time so the next team can play.

The management may ask anyone breaking these rules to leave the turf.
